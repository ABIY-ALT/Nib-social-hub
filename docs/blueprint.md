# **App Name**: BankSocialAI

## Core Features:

- AI Social Media Listening: Monitor social media for brand mentions, sentiment analysis, and trend detection using AI.
- Centralized Platform Connection: Securely connect and manage bank social media accounts (Facebook, X, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube) from a single dashboard.
- AI-Assisted Content Creation: Generate social media content (post captions, campaign messages, product announcements) in multiple languages using AI as a tool for brainstorming.
- Pre-Post Review & Compliance Check: Automatically review content for brand guidelines, regulatory compliance, and sensitive words before publishing.
- Scheduled & Controlled Publishing: Schedule posts across multiple platforms and maintain an audit trail for regulatory purposes.
- Analytics & Reporting: Track engagement, measure campaign performance, and provide AI-driven insights and recommendations.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: A deep blue (#1A237E) to convey trust, security, and professionalism, fitting for a banking application. It inspires confidence and reliability.
- Background color: A very light desaturated blue (#F0F2F9), creating a clean and calm backdrop.
- Accent color: A vibrant purple (#7B1FA2), positioned slightly to the left of blue on the color wheel, will be used to highlight key interactive elements and CTAs, adding a touch of creativity without compromising professionalism.
- Secondary color: Amber (#FFBF00) for highlighting important notifications or actions, providing a warm contrast to the primary blue.
- Headline font: 'Poppins', a geometric sans-serif for a precise, contemporary look.
- Body font: 'PT Sans', a humanist sans-serif combining modernity with warmth for readability.
- Use simple, professional icons to represent different features and social media platforms.
- Subtle animations to indicate loading, approval workflows, and key interactions.
